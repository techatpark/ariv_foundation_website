Ariv welcomes notes from learners, contributors, institutions, and people organising events. Say who you are, which part of the work you are writing about, and what you hope to do together.

## What to include

- Your name and, where you have one, your school, college, or organisation
- The area of work: [open source](/work/open-source-lab/), [DevCarnival](/work/devcarnival/), [TN eBooks](/work/tn-ebooks/), [CSE notes](/work/cse-notes/), [training](/work/training-centres/), [media](/work/public-media/), or a [gathering](/work/gatherings/)
- Whether you are writing as a learner, a contributor, or on behalf of an institution

## If you are learning

School material is on [Gurukulams, under Tamil Nadu Textbooks](https://gurukulams.com/publications/tnebooks/): 11th Physics, 12th Physics, and Vedic Maths. Read the project on the [TN eBooks](/work/tn-ebooks/) page. Computer science notes, including C programming, are on [Gurukulams, under Computer Science Notes](https://gurukulams.com/publications/csebooks/), and described on the [CSE notes](/work/cse-notes/) page.

## If you want to contribute

The [Open Source Lab](/work/open-source-lab/) is the maintained place for platforms and for people learning to contribute. [DevCarnival](https://devcarnival.com/) is the public gathering: 8–9 January 2027 in Chennai. Talks can be [submitted here](https://forms.gle/jgfKDj1hUCipcSTH7).

## If you represent an institution

Read [how collaboration is meant to work](/collaborate/), then write with a course, a cohort, or a research interest. A general enquiry is harder to answer than a specific one.
