Computer science notes are the foundation’s engineering-study materials: free notes for learners who should not need a large budget for books.

They are published on [Gurukulams → Computer Science Notes](https://gurukulams.com/publications/csebooks/). The catalogue describes itself as a collaborative set of computer science materials, open regardless of where a learner lives or what they can pay. The notes are organised as clear learning paths, certification-focused content, profession-specific resources, and a transition into modern programming.

## Available now

- **C Programming** — C programming notes

School textbooks for the Tamil Nadu state board are the sibling project, [TN eBooks](/work/tn-ebooks/).

A department that wants to add a subject, or use these notes inside a laboratory course, can do that as [collaboration](/collaborate/).
