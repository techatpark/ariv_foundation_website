[DevCarnival](https://devcarnival.com/) is the foundation’s technology gathering. Its own line for the event is “Technology. People. Purpose.”

The next edition is Friday 8 and Saturday 9 January 2027, at Infosys, Mahindra City, Chennai.

## The two days

What the event site publishes:

- **Parallel tech tracks.** Talks and workshops across AI, cloud-native technologies, backend and full-stack engineering, DevOps, data, system design, Java, and emerging work. Eight or more tracks, and fifty or more talks and workshops.
- **Tech Pavilion.** A hands-on floor of a hundred or more technology projects, developer tools, and working implementations. [TNEBooks](https://devcarnival.com/projects/tnebooks/) is one project on that floor.
- **A shared room.** Developers, technology communities, students, researchers, and people from industry. The site describes five audience spheres.

## How to take part

- [The event site](https://devcarnival.com/)
- [Register for a pass](https://devcarnival.com/#passport). A pass can cover day one, day two, or both days, including the pavilion. Pass holders receive a welcome kit, badge, and T-shirt.
- [Submit a talk](https://forms.gle/jgfKDj1hUCipcSTH7)
- [Tech Pavilion projects](https://devcarnival.com/projects/)
- [GitHub](https://github.com/devcarnival) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dev-carnival)

Infosys is the venue sponsor. Azul sponsors the T-shirts. MongoDB sponsors the goodies.

The gathering sits beside the [Open Source Lab](/work/open-source-lab/). The lab is where platforms are maintained. DevCarnival is where that work meets a wider room. Other conferences and workshops are described under [Gatherings](/work/gatherings/).
