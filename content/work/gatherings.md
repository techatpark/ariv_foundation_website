Conferences, seminars, and workshops are one of the company’s objects. They exist to advance technology and education, and to give people a room in which to learn together — including people who meet the foundation first at an event rather than in a classroom.

[DevCarnival](/work/devcarnival/) is the named technology gathering. This page is the wider object: conferences, seminars, workshops, and events of a similar kind.

## Tickets and the charitable objects

The company may generate resources for its charitable objects through reasonable conference ticket sales, subject to applicable law. A ticket is a way to sustain the work. It is not a reason to close the door on the first object, which is access irrespective of economic background. “Reasonable” is part of the object, and so is the law that governs it.

## What is scheduled

[DevCarnival](/work/devcarnival/) is the gathering with a date: Friday 8 and Saturday 9 January 2027, at Infosys, Mahindra City, Chennai. Talks, workshops, and the Tech Pavilion are on the [event site](https://devcarnival.com/), and passes are registered from [that site](https://devcarnival.com/#passport).

Institutions that want to host a seminar or a workshop with the foundation can write through the [collaboration](/collaborate/) page, and say who the learners are.
