The foundation produces and shares educational and awareness content on digital media, including YouTube and other online platforms, in service of its objects.

This is how work done in a lab, a classroom, or a workshop reaches people who cannot attend. A lesson, an explanation of a syllabus topic, or a public conversation about technology belongs here when it carries the company’s objects forward — equal access, open technology, affordable study, skill.

## What this media is for

- Lessons and explanations that a student can watch without paying for a coaching channel
- Awareness of the foundation’s initiatives, including [TN eBooks](/work/tn-ebooks/), the [Open Source Lab](/work/open-source-lab/), and [DevCarnival](/work/devcarnival/)
- A public record of gatherings, so a conference is not only available to the people in the room

A channel address will be published on this page when one is ready to share. Until then, the object stands: the company disseminates educational content. It does not treat media as a substitute for the curricula, the lab, or the training.
