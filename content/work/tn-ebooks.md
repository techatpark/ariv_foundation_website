TN eBooks is the foundation’s school-learning project. The company’s object for it is free or low-cost digital and gamified content, including the Standard XI and XII curricula.

The catalogue that is available now is on [Gurukulams](https://gurukulams.com/publications/tnebooks/). It is for Tamil Nadu government school learners, irrespective of socio-economic background, and it follows the Tamil Nadu curriculum. It also includes material for competitive exams such as NEET and JEE.

## Available now

On [Gurukulams → Tamil Nadu Textbooks](https://gurukulams.com/publications/tnebooks/):

- **11th Physics** — the physics textbook for 11th standard, Tamil Nadu state board
- **12th Physics** — the physics textbook for 12th standard, Tamil Nadu state board
- **Vedic Maths** — a guide for the Vedic maths classes from Sri Ranga Academy

Computer science notes are a separate catalogue: [CSE notes](/work/cse-notes/).

## The state portal, listed at DevCarnival

The [Tech Pavilion page for TNEBooks](https://devcarnival.com/projects/tnebooks/) describes the state portal at [tnebooks.tn.gov.in](https://tnebooks.tn.gov.in/). That listing says the portal is managed by the Tamil Nadu Text Book and Educational Services Corporation. It gives students, teachers, and competitive-exam aspirants free digital textbooks, teacher handbooks, and supplementary material from Class 1 to Class 12, in Tamil and English.

The same page describes a portal built for a slow connection: light PDF and ePub viewers, search in Tamil and English down to a chapter, and QR codes that connect a printed textbook to audio-visual material. It states a reach of more than 6 million students in Tamil Nadu schools.
